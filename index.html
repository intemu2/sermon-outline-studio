How it works

  1. You bring the passage. The app fetches the exact verse text so the outline is anchored in Scripture, not in a model’s memory.
  2. The outline engine drafts. Ten familiar passages have hand-written points; any other passage gets a faithful three-point structure built from its theme and your topic.
  3. You decide. Edit anything, verify every reference, then copy, download or print. The pastor preaches — the tool only drafts.

Faithfulness & responsible AI: AI can misquote Scripture and flatten meaning. That is why the verse text comes from a Bible API, the outline always shows its references, and nothing is presented as finished preaching. Human review is part of the workflow, not an afterthought. No congregant data is collected — everything is stored only in your own browser.
